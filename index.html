About

Just Him & the Guitar

Based in Milford, Michigan, Jacob Smith grew up in the small town of Byron, MI where music was always close by. He plays entirely acoustic - no backing tracks, no band, just a guitar and a room full of people. His sets move between original songs and covers that span rock, country, and folk, built for breweries, taprooms, and anywhere people gather for a good time and good music.
