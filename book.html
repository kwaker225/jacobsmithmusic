Frequently Asked Questions

Straight Answers Before You Reach Out

What does a booking include?

Just Jacob and his guitar - fully acoustic, no backing tracks, no band - plus professional sound and stage lighting provided by Uptown Light & Sound. He sings, plays, and reads the room, mixing originals with a wide range of covers spanning rock, country, and folk.

How is travel handled?

Jacob is based in Milford, MI and typically travels up to about 90 miles. Something further out? Just mention it in your request - he's happy to talk through it.

When is a date officially booked?

A form submission or message isn't a reservation on its own. Once you and Jacob agree on the details over the phone, text, email, or the form above, he'll confirm it directly with you.

Do you play recurring venue dates?

Yes - Jacob holds regular slots at several venues (see Where He's Played) and can set up a recurring booking at yours too.

Can Jacob play outdoors?

Absolutely. He's fully acoustic and doesn't need a stage or elaborate setup, so patios, decks, and outdoor events all work well - just let him know the setting when you reach out.

Can we request songs?

Yes, always. Jacob takes requests and builds sets that blend his originals with crowd-favorite covers - tell him what your crowd likes and he'll work it in.

Does Jacob provide sound equipment?

Yes - every show comes with professional sound and stage lighting, provided by Uptown Light & Sound and dialed in for the room, whether it's an intimate patio or a packed taproom.

How much space and power does Jacob need?

Just enough room for one performer plus his sound and lighting setup, and a couple of nearby outlets - no stage, no band rider.

What happens after I send a booking request?

Jacob personally reviews it - he doesn't have a booking agent or assistant screening requests - and replies within 24 hours with availability and next steps.

Can Jacob coordinate with our venue or event planner?

Of course - happy to work directly with whoever's running point on your event to sort out timing and logistics.
